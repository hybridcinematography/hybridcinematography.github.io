Woman original vs retime 2x2

Original and retimed reshoots at matched playback times. Top: captured footage and previs; red hatching marks missing coverage. Bottom: original timing and retimed generation. Retiming preserves the full path and 3.5-second duration, lingering in better-supported views and moving rapidly through poorly supported views to spend less time on details the model must invent. The overlays show estimated coverage and resolution demand, not measured hallucination error.
